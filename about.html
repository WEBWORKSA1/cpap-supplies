Helping CPAP Users Sleep Better Since 2024

We started CPAP.Supplies with one goal: give every CPAP user the unbiased information they need to get a great night's sleep, every night.

Our Mission

CPAP therapy changes lives — but only when it works properly. Too many users struggle with mask leaks, dry mouth, noisy machines, and confusing pressure settings, often without anyone to turn to for clear, trustworthy advice.

That is why we built CPAP.Supplies. We create evidence-based guides, publish honest product reviews, and deliver practical tips that help CPAP users overcome the everyday challenges of therapy. No sponsored rankings, no pay-to-play reviews — just straightforward information written for real people navigating real sleep apnea treatment.

What We Stand For

Six principles that guide every piece of content we publish and every recommendation we make.

Evidence-Based

Every recommendation is backed by clinical research, manufacturer data, and real-world testing by our editorial team.

Radically Independent

We never accept payment for reviews. Our recommendations are earned, not bought. Brands cannot influence our editorial decisions.

Plain Language

Always Current

Our Editorial Standards

The five pillars behind every article, review, and guide on CPAP.Supplies.

1

Independent Testing Protocol

Every product we review is tested hands-on by our team over a minimum two-week period. We evaluate comfort, noise levels, durability, ease of cleaning, and real-world performance — never relying solely on manufacturer claims.

2

Medical Review Process

Clinical and medical content is reviewed by qualified sleep health professionals before publication to ensure accuracy, safety, and alignment with current treatment guidelines.

3

Regular Content Audits

Every article is revisited on a quarterly cycle. We verify that product recommendations are still available, pricing is accurate, medical guidance reflects current standards, and links are functional.

4

Transparent Financial Disclosure

Affiliate links and advertising relationships are disclosed clearly at the top of every relevant page. We publish an annual transparency report detailing our revenue sources and editorial policies.

5

User Feedback Integration

Reader comments, survey responses, and direct emails shape our editorial calendar. When users report an issue with a recommendation, we investigate and update the content within 72 hours.
