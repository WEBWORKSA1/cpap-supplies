At CPAP.Supplies, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website cpap.supplies. Please read this policy carefully. By using our site, you agree to the practices described herein.

1 Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Subscribe to our email list or newsletter
  • Use our CPAP Supply Replacement Calculator
  • Fill out a contact form or send us an email
  • Leave a comment on a blog post

This information may include your name, email address, and any other details you choose to provide.

Usage Data

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing activity, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs
  • Date and time of access

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to collect usage data and improve your experience. See Section 3 for detailed information about our cookie practices.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Email Communications: To send you newsletters, CPAP supply replacement reminders, product recommendations, and updates you have opted into
  • Site Improvement: To understand how visitors use our site, identify areas for improvement, and optimize content and user experience
  • Analytics: To measure traffic patterns, page performance, and audience demographics via Google Analytics
  • Advertising: To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense and other advertising partners
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries, comments, or requests

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

3 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. We use the following types of cookies:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic features such as page navigation, remembering your cookie consent preferences, and maintaining session information. You cannot opt out of essential cookies as the site cannot function without them.

Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. These cookies collect information about page visits, time on site, traffic sources, and user behavior in an anonymized and aggregated form. Google Analytics may set cookies such as _ga, _gid, and _gat.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

Google uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to CPAP.Supplies and/or other sites on the Internet. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.

These cookies allow advertising networks to deliver ads that are more relevant to you and your interests. They track your browsing activity across websites and build a profile of your interests to show you targeted advertisements.

How to Manage Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored and delete individual cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies from specific sites
  • Block all cookies from being set
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Please note that blocking or deleting cookies may affect your experience on our website and limit certain functionality.

Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

  • Google Ads Personalization Settings
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Opt-Out Page

Third-party ad networks, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information about your browsing activity on our site and across other websites. This information is used to provide advertisements about goods and services that may be of interest to you.

Google uses the DART cookie to serve ads to users based on their visits to CPAP.Supplies and other sites on the Internet. The DART cookie is used by Google in the ads served on publisher websites displaying AdSense for content ads.

CPAP.Supplies has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers. You should consult the respective privacy policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information on their practices.

CPAP.Supplies is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
